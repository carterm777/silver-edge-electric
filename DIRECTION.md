# Design direction — Silver Edge Electric

Assigned so that no two of the 25 sites in this campaign converge on the same
look. These are **starting positions with authority**, not idle suggestions: build
from them. You may deviate from any one of them, but the deviation costs you one
line of written justification in `SELECTION-LOG.md` explaining what the assigned
option lost against and why — the same discipline `section-style-repo.md` asks
for everywhere else.

**Primary style family:** minimal-clean
**Page ambition level:** restrained-and-quiet — precision is the whole point

## The specific risk on this brief

The palette is greyscale with no hue at all. That is a gift, not a problem: this is the one site in the batch that can be genuinely monochrome and luxurious. Everything rides on type, spacing and one material accent. Get the spacing scale perfect; there is nothing to hide behind.

## Signature element — the photo-diagnosis widget

Full-Bleed Immersive Widget — the uploader occupies its own quiet field with one accent hairline

This is the most designed thing on the page. If a stranger screenshotted one
element from this site, it should be this one.

## Carry these through the page

- **Image technique to feature:** Framed or Bordered Photo Insert — from `references/image-and-visual-richness.md`. Use it at least twice, deliberately.
- **Motion technique to feature:** Depth Settle on every photograph — from `references/animation-and-motion-richness.md`.

Both are on top of, not instead of, the per-section choices above. Read the real
entry in the reference file before implementing either — the Avoid-when field
applies to you.
